About Martin Zahn

I’m a video creator, editor, and motion graphics artist from Cincinnati, Ohio. I like being hands-on through the whole process of making a video—whether I’m filming interviews on location, running live studio equipment, cutting a story together in Premiere Pro, or animating graphics in After Effects.

Over the past few years, I’ve put together local feature stories, promotional videos, and a collaborative short film at the Ohio University High School Media Workshop. For the last three years, I’ve also been part of the crew behind Braves Beat, helping produce and switch live school news broadcasts.
